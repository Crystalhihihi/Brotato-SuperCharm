【标题】超级魅惑 SuperCharm

【简介（贴在创意工坊描述）】

魅惑流全面强化：永久魅惑、魅惑大军、boss 收编、复活币。

Vanilla charm, unchained: permanent charm, a real charmed army, boss taming, and a new item.

▌功能 / Features

- 魅惑不再 8 秒后死亡，小怪魅惑瞬间回满血
- 魅惑怪会被敌人锁定和攻击（魅惑后 1.5 秒保护期），你的武器不会误伤它们
- 魅惑怪继承你的击退属性，并每秒回复等同于你「生命恢复」的血量
- boss/精英死亡时 10% 概率魅惑复活（25% 血量、数值冻结在死亡波次），活着就能带进下一波
- 魅惑大军会吸引额外 boss：魅惑方战力越强，来袭的 boss 越多（有上限，无尽模式随波数放宽）
- 新道具「复活币」：随机绑定一种被魅惑的小怪，之后每波它都会满血魅惑参战，死了下波再来；诅咒版绑定两只

- Charm no longer expires after 8s; charmed enemies refill to full HP
- Charmed enemies are targeted and attacked by enemies (1.5s grace after charm); your weapons never hit them
- Charmed enemies inherit your knockback and regenerate HP equal to your HP Regeneration
- Bosses/elites have a 10% chance to revive charmed on death (25% HP, stats frozen at that wave) and follow you to the next wave while alive
- Your charm swarm attracts extra bosses: the stronger the swarm, the more bosses show up (capped; scales up in endless)
- New item "Revival Coin": binds a random charmed enemy species; it joins every wave charmed at full HP, even if it dies. Binds two when cursed
